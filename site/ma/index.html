How to use it

  • Arrow button shows where you are and lists the closest pins.
  • Walk button turns on alerts. Your phone buzzes and a red banner shows when you get within your alert distance of a pin. Keep the app open while you walk.
  • Search any street to check a route before you head out.
  • Hot spots shows a heat map and ranks the busiest areas in view: the most people listed within a quarter mile. Move the map and the ranking follows.
  • Dashed circles are people the registry lists without a street address (homeless, a PO box or none at all). They sit at the town center, not at a real spot, so walk alerts and Hot spots leave them out. Pins on the right street whose exact house is unknown do count.
  • Tap a pin, then See who to open that person's page on the official state registry.
  • On a phone, use your browser's Add to Home Screen to install it like an app.

About the data

Pins mark a building, not a unit. Apartment buildings, shelters and workplaces can hold many people who are not on any registry. Use this for awareness of where to be extra watchful, nothing more.

Always confirm current information on the official MA SORB public registry. Registry information changes often.

Use it responsibly

Do not approach, confront or contact anyone at these addresses. If you see something concerning, call 911.

One quick step first

The state registry shows a person's photo and details only after you agree to its terms. It asks about once per visit.

  1. Tap Open the registry. Tick the box, do the "I'm not a robot" check and tap to agree.
  2. Come back to this tab and tap Show this person.

If a See who button ever lands on the registry's home page, the state's session timed out. Agree again there, then tap See who again.
