How it works

Walk alerts

Keep the map open while you walk. Your phone buzzes and a red banner shows when you get within 150, 300 or 500 feet of a pin.

Check a route first

Search any street or address. The street lights up and the list shows what is on it and within a quarter mile.

Hot spots

A heat map and a ranked list of the busiest areas, so you can plan which neighborhoods to walk.

Data comes from public state sex offender registries (Massachusetts: the Sex Offender Registry Board public search). Only Level 2 and Level 3 listings are public and shown. This site stores no names or photos; each pin links to the person's page on the official registry. Information changes often, so always confirm on the official registry.

Use it for awareness only. Using registry information to commit a crime, or to harass or discriminate against anyone, is illegal (M.G.L. c. 6, section 178N). Do not approach or contact anyone at these addresses. If you see something concerning, call 911.

SafeWalk is an independent project and is not affiliated with any state agency.
